PROVIDER-NEUTRAL

The research is larger than one provider.

Applications target a typed decision boundary. Hosted, local, general-model, and deterministic baselines can then be compared without rewriting downstream logic.

METHOD

Evidence first.

Freeze conditions before scored output, preserve first-run evidence, version corrections explicitly, and analyze failure modes before optimizing.
